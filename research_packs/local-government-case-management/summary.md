# Digital case-management services for local government

Public-sector case management procurement is governed by the Procurement Act 2023, shifting from MEAT to a Most Advantageous Tender (MAT) approach that emphasizes whole-life value, risk mitigation, and demonstrable social benefit. Successful delivery requires aligning software workflows with statutory requirements using no-code, API-first architectures rather than legacy-heavy customization. Projects frequently fail due to flawed data migration, poor governance, and the adaptation trap, where custom code bloat prevents upgrades and inflates costs. Bidders must provide transparent TCO, measurable social value, and robust, compliant integration plans.
