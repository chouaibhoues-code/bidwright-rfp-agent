# Digital case-management services for local government

Digital case management transformation in local government requires navigating complex operational risk profiles and statutory compliance. Successful projects prioritize the adoption of standard software processes over heavy customization, which is a primary driver of transformation failure. Bids must demonstrate robust technical and operational integrity, specifically regarding data migration and financial control. The implementation must include automated, non-alterable audit trails and segregation of duties. Procurement evaluation has shifted toward the Most Advantageous Tender (MAT) framework, necessitating clear, measurable, and reportable commitments to Social Value. Organizations must balance these procurement imperatives with rigorous testing regimes, such as parallel running, to ensure service continuity.
