# Electronic signature services in higher education

Higher education e-signature procurement requires technical integration with academic identity federations (InCommon, Shibboleth, SAML 2.0) and rigorous adherence to FERPA regulations for data governance. Bidders must navigate the consolidated HECVAT 4 assessment framework, replacing older standalone questionnaires and the retired Community Broker Index. Architecturally, institutions require webhook-driven event processing for real-time synchronization with SIS/ERP systems, prioritizing idempotent middleware to handle events asynchronously without violating API rate limits or creating data duplicates.
