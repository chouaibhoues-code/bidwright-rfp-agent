# Assumptions

What the design takes as true, what breaks if it is false, how it is checked, and what happens if the check fails. Check ids refer to the research register in `context/Research-Register.md`, from Iteration 0b.

| Assumed | Breaks if false | Checked how | If false, then |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free-tier limits roughly match the community figures. Checked 2026-11-02 in AI Studio: Flash models allow 20 requests a day (the low end of the community 20-40), 3.5 Flash-Lite 500, and Gemma 4 14,400 at 16K input tokens a minute | Capacity and the paid budget | V-03 and the owner's AI Studio check | Recount capacity, and bring the paid-budget decision forward |
| Gemini 3 models need no sampling parameters: 3.6 and later ignore them, and Google advises keeping defaults on earlier Gemini 3 models such as 3.5 Flash-Lite | Decoding settings | C7-01 | Set the documented values per model in `config/models.yaml` |
| `docling-serve` keeps text exact enough for quote checks. Checked 2026-11-04 by S-01: Docling located all 66 visible hero gold clauses in their own documents, and pdfplumber 55, losing the two-column section | The parser choice | S-01 and S-02 | pdfplumber stays the PDF parser, and DR-04 picks a fallback for tables |
| A local demo on free-tier Gemini with synthetic data is acceptable under Google's terms. This is a legal reading, not a settled fact. | The data rule | Read 2026-11-02 (V-03): free-tier content may be used to improve Google's products and read by human reviewers, so no sensitive, confidential or personal information goes in, and an app offered to users in the EEA, Switzerland or the UK must use paid services. The corpus is synthetic, the demo replays recorded responses and calls no API, and live runs use the runner's own key | Record the demo on `local` or on the paid tier, and use a paid key for live runs offered to users in those regions |
| A model family other than Gemini is available for `assess`, `critic` and judges from Iteration 3 | The family rule for assessment and review | Read 2026-11-02 (V-03): Cerebras has no permanent free tier; its trial is $5 of credits, given once a payment method is added, which expire 30 days after they are granted | Create the Cerebras key at the start of Iteration 3, so the 30 days cover its runs; beyond that, paid usage within the paid budget set at the Iteration 1 exit, $40 for Iterations 2 and 3 (D-01), or Groq for small calls |
| Google's 429 for a spent daily quota names a per-day quota id in its details. Since ADR-029 it is the only stop on the free tier, where the limiter counts but no longer caps the day | A spent free quota looks like a per-minute limit: the call is retried 3 times, then moves to the fallback model, or faults a matrix run instead of pausing it. Nothing is spent and no output is wrong, but the run ends in the wrong status | The Gemini adapter matches quota ids that contain `PerDay`, the form of the free tier's daily quota ids (`test_adapters.py`); ADR-029 accepts the risk | Restore a local daily cap on `free` with `daily: enforce`, the condition ADR-029 names for its reversal |
| Synthetic RFPs stress the pipeline like real ones | What the scores mean | DR-10, and a realism check against QuRE | Raise the corpus's length and depth, and state the gap under known limits |
| The hero package's procurement regime, the fictional Halverton Public Contracts Rules 2025 with an open procedure modeled on Directive 2014/24/EU, is close enough to a real public tender to test instructions, eligibility, evaluation factors, an addendum and an order of precedence. The spec cites no real legislation. | What the hero's instruction, eligibility and precedence clauses test | Not checked against the Directive's text, by design: the corpus names no real law | State the gap under known limits, and base a later corpus RFP on a published procedure |
| Different model families reduce correlated errors. Published results also report errors that persist across vendors. | The independence of `assess` and `redteam` | Q-04, before Iteration 3 | Limit `assess` to entailment over the cited text with code pre-checks, report joint error, and spot-check 10 assessments by hand |
| 500 words approximates one page | Page-limit checks | Not checked | Use the RFP's own page measure where it states one |
| An hour means focused elapsed work by the owner and Claude together | `TIMELOG.md` | Not checked | Redefine the unit in `TIMELOG.md` |
| RFPs are in English | Language scope | Not checked | Study the language scope and state it |
| Whole-RFP inputs stay synthetic, and licensed public research datasets are acceptable for stage benchmarks | External benchmark runs | License checks (V-02), then runs through `bidwright-eval external` only | Drop that benchmark and record why |
| `/inputs` was meant to hold an RFP brief, possibly a short one, rather than a 20-25-page package | Input-size design and the `free` corpus rule | Not checked: the owner chose not to ask | A one-page brief written by the owner runs on `--profile local` at release, reported separately |
| The assignment's "using an LLM via Copilot/Cursor/opencode/Ollama" allows any free tier, as its ground rules suggest | The scored model path | Not checked | Ollama carries the scored path. At release, the `local` profile completes the thin thread on the development RFP. |
| In an RFP, "should" states a preference, not a requirement, so a "should" clause is `OPTIONAL` and never counts as a missed mandatory requirement | Missed-mandatory counts, and the extract prompt's decision table | The development RFP's gold uses this convention; real tenders vary | Make "should" depend on the RFP's own definition of its terms, which `candidates` collects in the term registry and each `extract` pack carries from Iteration 1 |
| `gemini-3.8-flash` on the free tier answers within the call timeout | Every `reason` node: approach, risk and draft | Two live runs on 2026-11-02 got 504s on every attempt, at 60 s and at 120 s, and a later probe got 429 RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED once the 20-a-day quota was spent | Done 2026-11-02: the `free` profile's `reason` role moved to `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` (ADR-003), and Flash stays for quality runs |

## Regulation

As of 2026-10-30, from secondary sources not yet checked against the Official Journal: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 defers the AI Act's standalone high-risk duties to 2027-12-02, and Article 50's transparency duties apply from 2026-08-02. No design decision depends on this. Bidwright drafts only, submits nothing, and labels every output as an unreviewed AI draft.
