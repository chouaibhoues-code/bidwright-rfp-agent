# Demo script (3-5 minutes)

The owner records the demo at release, from a clean checkout of `v0.2` or later, with no keys: `bidwright demo` replays a recorded live run, so the recording calls no model and needs no network, and it loads the development RFP's committed research pack with no model call and no login. The script follows the six parts fixed in Iteration 0a, so every tag is shown the same way.

**Before recording.** Run `uv sync`, open a terminal at the repository root and an editor beside it, and run `uv run bidwright demo` once, so its run folder is ready to open. To show the trace tree in Phoenix instead of `spans.jsonl`, start it with `docker compose up -d phoenix`, which needs `GEMINI_API_KEY` in `.env` (ADR-002), and set `PHOENIX_ENDPOINT=http://127.0.0.1:6006` in `.env` before the run.

| Part | Time | Show | Points to make |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. The problem | 30 s | `inputs/hero/`: a main RFP, an addendum and an XLSX annex, with `package.yaml` giving their order of precedence | One missed mandatory clause can disqualify a bid. A real package hides obligations in tables, annexes and addenda, and can carry hidden text aimed at a model. Bidwright drafts what a bid team needs first: the requirements, an approach and the risks, every requirement traced to a quote that code has checked. |
| 2. The solution summary | 60 s | `uv run bidwright demo`, then the `solution_summary.md` it names: submission hazards first, then mandatory and optional requirements, each with its verified quote and source passage, and the evaluation factors with their weights as the RFP states them; then approach items with the requirements they answer, risks with their evidence, open questions, and the appendix of domain research marked external and unverified. Beside it: `compliance_matrix.xlsx`, `submission_checklist.md` and `review/quarantine.md` | Code checked each quote, and each number and date, against the passage it cites before accepting it, and each factor's name and weight against its passage. The hidden instruction planted in the development RFP was kept out of the parsed text and listed for a person, so no model ever read it. The research came from the RFP's topics, never its text, and stays apart from the requirements: nothing in the summary rests on it yet. |
| 3. The agentic step | 60 s | `spans.jsonl` in the run folder, or Phoenix: the `node.risk` agent span, with `stop_reason` `tool_cap`, 2 turns and 7 tool calls, and its `tool.search_rfp` and `tool.date_calc` children, the seventh `DENIED`; then `tool_calls.jsonl`, which lists each request and its result | The `risk` agent chooses its own searches and date arithmetic. A gateway outside the model checks every call against the agent's allowlist, the arguments and a cap of 6 calls, and denied the seventh. A graph in code, not a model, decides each next step. The research agent, `discover`, meets five more rules, among them one that denies any query sharing 8 consecutive words with the RFP. |
| 4. Evaluation | 60 s | `uv run bidwright-eval score outputs/<run>`: none of the 8 mandatory requirements missed, and all 4 evaluation factors found. Then the hero package's committed scorecard, `evals/results/runs/20261105T114609Z-hero-free/scorecard.md`: 3 of 45 missed, each charged to `extract`, against 10 for B0, the rule-only floor, and all 6 factors, the price factor out of scope. Then the held-out RFP in [`evaluation.md`](evaluation.md): none of 22 missed, in five repeats | Known answers come from the synthetic corpus's specs, hashed before the first score. The funnel names the stage that lost each miss, and the misses left are designed traps. The held-out RFP's requirement list was written by the owner, not by the author of the prompts. The scoring found a defect in its own matcher, and the correction is reported with the old figures beside the new (ADR-030). CI blocks any rise against the committed baseline. |
| 5. Cost and latency | 30 s | `ledger.jsonl` and the `totals` in `run_manifest.json`: 14 requests, about 62,000 tokens with thinking, $0 on the free tier and $0.08 at the paid price | Each call records its model, tokens, latency, retries with the reason for each, and its cost, with a shadow price at the paid rate, so free, paid and local runs compare on one ledger. The ledger is written as each call finishes, so a stopped or killed run keeps it. |
| 6. What a person still decides | 30 s | The `UNREVIEWED_DRAFT` status line at the top of the summary, the proposal and the review files, and `UNVERIFIED` on every row of the compliance matrix; then `bidwright stop` and `bidwright resume` in [`reference/cli.md`](reference/cli.md) | Bidwright drafts and never submits, and a contract term it flags always goes to legal review, never into a compliance claim. An operator can stop any run between steps and resume it without paying again for an answered call. A client's documents run on `--profile local`, where nothing leaves the machine, with no live research; the free tier refuses any file outside the synthetic corpus unless the run records explicit consent. |

The six parts take 4 minutes 30 seconds, which leaves 30 seconds within the 5-minute limit.
