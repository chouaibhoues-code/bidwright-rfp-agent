# Proposal draft: Request for Proposal: Electronic Signature Service

Status: UNREVIEWED_DRAFT. Every requirement below quotes the RFP, and a person must review this draft before anyone relies on it.

## Our understanding of the College's needs

Proposals must be submitted through the College's procurement portal as a single PDF of no more than 20 pages by 2026-12-01 12:00 UTC [REQ-0001]. The Supplier shall keep an audit record of every signature event [REQ-0002] and support signing on current desktop and mobile browsers [REQ-0003]. Reminders must be sent to each signer who has not signed within 72 hours [REQ-0004]. The Supplier should provide templates for the twelve most used forms [REQ-0005] and store signed documents for ten years [REQ-0006]. The Supplier should migrate signed records held in the current tool by 2027-02-28 [REQ-0007]. The service must integrate with the College's student records system through its existing REST API [REQ-0008]. Bidders may propose an on-premises deployment option [REQ-0009]. The service shall be available 99.5% of the time in each calendar month, excluding planned maintenance announced five working days in advance [REQ-0010]. The Supplier should respond to severity-one incidents within one hour, at any time of day [REQ-0011]. Finally, the service must be live for students by 2027-01-11, the first day of spring enrollment [REQ-0012].

## Our approach

We will assemble the proposal as a single PDF of no more than 20 pages and submit it through the College's procurement portal by 2026-12-01 12:00 UTC, noting that the submission timing is an assumption [APP-001]. We support signing on current desktop and mobile browsers, keep an audit record of every signature event including signer identity, time, and document version, and store signed documents for ten (10) years, with the storage location being an assumption [APP-002]. We send a reminder to each signer who has not signed within 72 hours and integrate with the College's student records system through its existing REST API, treating API credentials as an assumption [APP-003]. We ensure the service is live for students by 2027-01-11, the first day of spring enrollment, and maintain 99.5% availability in each calendar month excluding planned maintenance announced five working days in advance, with maintenance scheduling details being an assumption [APP-004].

## Risks and assumptions

The main risk is that the final assembled proposal draft exceeds 20 pages or the upload starts too late, causing rejection and disqualification [RSK-001]. We enforce an 18-page limit on draft sections and upload a working draft to the procurement portal 24 hours before the 2026-12-01 deadline [RSK-001]. A second risk is that active student record lookups during spring enrollment need historical data not yet migrated, causing staff and students to check two systems and leading to retrieval errors and compliance breaches [RSK-002]. We prioritize migrating active student cohorts before the 2027-01-11 go-live date and implement a unified search interface bridging the new service and current tool [RSK-002]. Open questions concern whether templates for the College's twelve most used forms should be included and their format [Q-01]; the format of signed records in the current tool and expected migration volume by 2027-02-28 [Q-02]; infrastructure and licensing assumptions for the optional on-premises deployment [Q-03]; whether the one-hour response time for severity-one incidents applies 24/7 and through which communication channel [Q-04]; expected data volume and schema format for signed records to migrate by 2027-02-28 [Q-05]; and rate limits or concurrent connection caps for the College's existing REST API [Q-06].
