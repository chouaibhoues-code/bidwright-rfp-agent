# Our understanding of the College's needs

The College requires each bidder to submit its proposal through the procurement portal as a single PDF of at most 20 pages by 2026-12-01 12:00 UTC [REQ-0001]. The service must go live for students by 2027-01-11, the first day of spring enrollment [REQ-0012]. The Supplier keeps an audit record of every signature event, including identity, time, and document version [REQ-0002], supports signing on current desktop and mobile browsers [REQ-0003], sends a reminder to each signer who has not signed within 72 hours [REQ-0004], and stores signed documents for ten years [REQ-0006]. Furthermore, the service must integrate with the College's student records system through its existing REST API [REQ-0008] and remain available 99.5% of the time in each calendar month, excluding planned maintenance announced five working days in advance [REQ-0010]. The Supplier should provide templates for the twelve most used forms [REQ-0005], migrate signed records held in the current tool by 2027-02-28 [REQ-0007], and respond to severity-one incidents within one hour at any time of day [REQ-0011]. A bidder may propose an on-premises deployment option [REQ-0009].
