# How we would deliver

We assemble the proposal as a single PDF of at most 20 pages and submit it through the College's procurement portal by 2026-12-01 12:00 UTC, ensuring the service is live for students by 2027-01-11 [APP-001]. We support signing on current desktop and mobile browsers, and send a reminder to each signer who has not signed within 72 hours [APP-002]. We maintain an audit record of every signature event including signer identity, time, and document version, and store signed documents for ten years [APP-003]. We integrate the service with the College's student records system through its existing REST API [APP-004]. We ensure the service is available 99.5% of the time in each calendar month, excluding planned maintenance announced five working days in advance [APP-005].
