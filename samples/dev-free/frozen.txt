Request for Proposal: Electronic Signature Service

Kestrel Vale Community College

Reference KV-2026-014. Issued 2026-11-03.

This is a fictional document written to test software. Kestrel Vale Community College is not a real
organization.

1 Introduction

Kestrel Vale Community College (the College) invites proposals for a cloud electronic signature
service. The service replaces paper forms for enrollment, financial aid and staff contracts. The
College serves about 6,400 students on two campuses and employs 410 staff.

2 Background

Since 2019 the College has collected signatures with a hosted forms tool whose contract ends on
2027-03-31. About 38,000 signed records are held in that tool.

Previous suppliers have generally held ISO 27001 certification.

The College plans to replace its student records system during 2027, and the replacement has not
yet been chosen. The College is closed from 2026-12-19 to 2027-01-04, and no staff are available
for testing or training in that period.

3 Instructions to bidders

This section explains how to respond.

Proposals must be submitted through the College's procurement portal as a single PDF of no more
than 20 pages by 2026-12-01 12:00 UTC.

Section 7 describes how proposals are scored.

4 Requirements

The requirements in this section apply for the whole contract term.

4.1 Signing

The Supplier shall keep an audit record of every signature event, including the signer's identity, the
time and the document version.

The Supplier shall:

(a) support signing on current desktop and mobile browsers;

(b) send a reminder to each signer who has not signed within 72 hours.

The Supplier should provide templates for the College's twelve most used forms.

4.2 Records

The Supplier shall store signed documents for ten (10) years. The Supplier should migrate the
signed records held in the current tool by 2027-02-28.

4.3 Integration

The service must integrate with the College's student records system through its existing REST API.

Bidders may propose an on-premises deployment option.

5 Service levels

The service shall be available 99.5% of the time in each calendar month, excluding planned
maintenance announced five working days in advance.

The Supplier should respond to severity-one incidents within one hour, at any time of day.

6 Timeline

Contract award is planned for 2026-12-15. The service must be live for students by 2027-01-11, the
first day of spring enrollment.

7 Evaluation

Proposals are scored on the criteria below.

Criterion | Weight
Technical approach | 40%
Service levels and support | 20%
Integration and migration | 20%
Price | 20%

8 Contract

The contract runs for three years from award, with an option to extend it by one year.
