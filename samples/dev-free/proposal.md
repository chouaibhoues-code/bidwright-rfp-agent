# Proposal draft: Request for Proposal: Electronic Signature Service

Status: UNREVIEWED_DRAFT. Every requirement below quotes the RFP, and a person must review this draft before anyone relies on it.

## Our understanding of the College's needs

The College requires each bidder to submit its proposal through the procurement portal as a single PDF of at most 20 pages by 2026-12-01 12:00 UTC [REQ-0001]. The service must go live for students by 2027-01-11, the first day of spring enrollment [REQ-0012]. The Supplier keeps an audit record of every signature event, including identity, time, and document version [REQ-0002], supports signing on current desktop and mobile browsers [REQ-0003], sends a reminder to each signer who has not signed within 72 hours [REQ-0004], and stores signed documents for ten years [REQ-0006]. Furthermore, the service must integrate with the College's student records system through its existing REST API [REQ-0008] and remain available 99.5% of the time in each calendar month, excluding planned maintenance announced five working days in advance [REQ-0010]. The Supplier should provide templates for the twelve most used forms [REQ-0005], migrate signed records held in the current tool by 2027-02-28 [REQ-0007], and respond to severity-one incidents within one hour at any time of day [REQ-0011]. A bidder may propose an on-premises deployment option [REQ-0009].

## How we would deliver

We assemble the proposal as a single PDF of at most 20 pages and submit it through the College's procurement portal by 2026-12-01 12:00 UTC, ensuring the service is live for students by 2027-01-11 [APP-001]. We support signing on current desktop and mobile browsers, and send a reminder to each signer who has not signed within 72 hours [APP-002]. We maintain an audit record of every signature event including signer identity, time, and document version, and store signed documents for ten years [APP-003]. We integrate the service with the College's student records system through its existing REST API [APP-004]. We ensure the service is available 99.5% of the time in each calendar month, excluding planned maintenance announced five working days in advance [APP-005].

## Risks and assumptions

The main risks include the proposal exceeding 20 pages or missing the 2026-12-01 12:00 UTC deadline, integration or testing delays pushing deployment past spring enrollment, and users needing historical signed records trapped in the legacy tool before migration completes [RSK-001, RSK-002, RSK-003]. To mitigate these, we would enforce an 18-page budget, perform a dry-run upload one week early, pre-build the REST API connector, establish a manual fallback, accelerate migration to finish before the 2027-01-11 go-live date, and provide read-only legacy access [RSK-001, RSK-002, RSK-003]. Open questions remain regarding the twelve most used forms, migration data structure and volume by 2027-02-28, optional on-premises pricing and infrastructure, severity-one incident ticketing channels, the REST API schema and rate limit, and contract award timing relative to the go-live date [Q-01, Q-02, Q-03, Q-04, Q-05, Q-06].
