# Proposal draft: Request for Proposal: Electronic Signature Service

Status: UNREVIEWED_DRAFT. Every requirement below quotes the RFP, and a person must review this draft before anyone relies on it.

## Our understanding of the College's needs

Each bidder submits its proposal through the College's procurement portal as a single PDF of at most 20 pages by 2026-12-01 12:00 UTC [REQ-0002]. The service must be live for students by 2027-01-11, the first day of spring enrollment [REQ-0014]. The Supplier keeps an audit record of every signature event, including the signer's identity, the time, and the document version [REQ-0003], and supports signing on current desktop and mobile browsers [REQ-0004]. Reminders are sent to each signer who has not signed within 72 hours [REQ-0005], and signed documents are stored for ten years [REQ-0007]. The service must integrate with the College's student records system through its existing REST API [REQ-0009], and shall be available 99.5% of the time in each calendar month, excluding planned maintenance announced five working days in advance [REQ-0011]. Additionally, the Supplier should provide templates for the College's twelve most used forms [REQ-0006], migrate the signed records held in the current tool by 2027-02-28 [REQ-0008], and respond to severity-one incidents within one hour, at any time of day [REQ-0012]. Bidders may propose an on-premises deployment option [REQ-0010].

## Our approach

We assemble the proposal as a single PDF of at most 20 pages and submit it via the procurement portal before 2026-12-01 12:00 UTC, with the service going live for students by the first day of spring enrollment on 2027-01-11 [APP-001]; portal reliability is assumed [APP-001]. We maintain an audit record capturing signer identity, time, and document version for every signature event, and store signed documents for ten years [APP-002]. We support signing across current desktop and mobile browsers and send an automated reminder to any signer who has not completed signing within 72 hours [APP-003]. We integrate the service with the College's student records system using its existing REST API and maintain 99.5% monthly availability excluding planned maintenance announced five working days in advance [APP-004].

## Risks and assumptions

Integration delays past 2027-01-11 and staff being unable to access historical signed records during spring enrollment present the main risks, which would breach contract terms and cause administrative delays [RSK-001, RSK-002]. We would address API integration hurdles by requesting API documentation and sandbox access prior to contract award and preparing a fallback manual roster import mechanism [RSK-001]. We would address migration timing by prioritizing the migration of the most frequently accessed form templates before 2027-01-11 and providing staff with clear documentation on how to access legacy archives [RSK-002]. This matters to ensure the service launches for students on the first day of spring enrollment and to prevent compliance confusion [RSK-001, RSK-002]. The following open questions remain: whether templates for the College's twelve most used forms should be included and their specific formats [Q-01]; migration scope and data volume by 2027-02-28 [Q-02]; optional on-premises deployment pricing and architecture [Q-03]; support channels and escalation paths for severity-one incidents [Q-04]; expected schema and rate limit for the REST API [Q-05]; and whether a phased rollout is allowed for the 2027-01-11 target go-live date [Q-06].
